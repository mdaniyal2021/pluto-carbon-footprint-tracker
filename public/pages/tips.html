Frequently Asked Questions
Can I edit my logged entries? +
Yes — open the Emission Log page, find the entry you wish to remove, and click the Delete button. You can then re-add it with the correct values.
Do I need to log every day? +
No. Log activities whenever you like. Even occasional tracking gives you useful insights over time. The more consistent you are, the more accurate your trend data will be.
How is my carbon footprint calculated? +
Each activity type has a published emission factor (kg CO₂ per unit). For example, driving 1 km in a petrol car produces approximately 0.21 kg of CO₂. Your quantity is multiplied by that factor to produce the total.
Is my data secure? +
Passwords are hashed using bcrypt before storage and are never stored in plain text. All session data is managed server-side. The application complies with GDPR principles — your data is yours.
What is the daily CO₂ average per person? +
The global average is approximately 26 kg of CO₂ per person per day, though this varies widely by country and lifestyle. Pluto CFT helps you understand where you stand relative to this benchmark.
